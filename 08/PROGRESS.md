### Notes
- EGFR (Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor)- highly specialized molecules (kinase inhibitors) targeted for cancer research
- SM (Small molecules): chemically synthesized organic compounds with a very low molecular weight
- LM (Large molecules): extrated or synthesized using living organisms like cell cultures, bacteria or yeast. Highly complex, high-molecular-weight compounds
- Tarceva (erlotinib) is a small molecule kinase inhibitor
- Vabysmo (faricimab) is a large molecule monoclonal antibody
