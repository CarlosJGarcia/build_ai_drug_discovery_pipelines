### Notes

Molecule: the smallest unit of a chemical substance that has all the properties of that substance. It is formed by two or more atoms held together by chemical bonds.

Chemistry:
- Inorganic
- Organic
    - Petrochemicals (Fuels)
    - Synthetic polymers (Plastics, nylon, polyester)
    - Agro and industrial (Pesticides, solvents, dyes, flavorings)
    - Pharmaceuticals
- Biochemistry (living organisms)
